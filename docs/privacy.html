First Light Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

The short version: your alarms stay on your phone. First Light has no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and it never sees your camera images.

What stays on your phone

Your alarms, settings, wake-up barcode number and wake history are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. We don't run servers, so there is nowhere for them to go.

Camera

First Light uses the camera only when you register or scan your wake-up barcode. Each camera frame is read on your phone to find the barcode, then thrown away. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere. The app keeps only the number printed under the barcode.

The barcode scanner (Google ML Kit)

Barcodes are read by Google's ML Kit, which runs through Google Play services on your phone. ML Kit may send Google limited diagnostic and usage information to keep the scanner working well, such as device and app information, performance metrics and error codes. Google says this is encrypted in transit and not shared with third parties. It does not include your camera images or barcode contents. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure for details.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, a copy of your alarms and settings is included in your phone's backup to your own Google account, so they come back when you set up a new phone or reinstall. This is handled by Android and Google under your account. We can't see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Purchases

First Light Plus is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details, only whether Plus is unlocked.

Permissions, and why

  • Alarms and reminders: to ring at the exact minute you set.
  • Notifications and full-screen alerts: to show the ringing screen, even on the lock screen.
  • Camera: to scan your wake-up barcode, as described above.
  • Run at startup: to set your alarms again after your phone restarts.
  • Vibration and keeping the screen awake: so the alarm actually wakes you.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking of our own.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your information.

Children

First Light is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Everything First Light stores is on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes it. To remove a backup copy, manage your backups in your Google account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
